# Instructions for the research agent

Describe your research here; the agent reads this before every answer. The graph schema (five levels:
method families, techniques, papers, claims, quantities) and its rules are fixed and documented in
docs/GRAPH_SCHEMA.md of the litgraph repository; what you write here is the field-specific part:

- The research question and why it matters; which quantities "accurate" refers to.
- Terminology rules: which names to use, what to define, what to keep apart (measured vs interpreted).
- What counts as a paper worth adding, and what does not (e.g. single-material application papers).
- Which families the field has, if you already know them, so the agent files papers consistently.
